🔒 S&A Doc Scan · Privacy Policy

Applies to: S&A Doc Scan (scan.soramma.app) and its iPhone app. Last updated: November 2026.

English

Short version: S&A Doc Scan keeps your scanned documents in your own private library so you can open them on your devices. We do not show ads, do not sell your data and do not track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, your phone number and a password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it.
  • Your scans. The pictures you scan or upload, the text and fields found in them, and their dates. They are stored in your private library and are visible only to you (and to the service administrator for support).
  • Business details (optional). Business name, details and logo if you add them, used on documents you create.
  • Sign-in information. A sign-in token and a short device label (for example "iPhone · Safari") so you can see and manage where you are signed in.
  • Support messages. Messages you send in the in-app support chat.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. A picture is saved only if you keep the scan. The app does not use your location, contacts or microphone.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling or sharing of your data with advertisers. Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with our hosting provider (Cloudflare).

Delete your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open the menu and tap Delete my account. Your account, scans, support messages and sign-ins are then permanently deleted. You can also delete single scans at any time.

Children

The app is for general office and business use and is not directed at children.

Contact

Use the in-app Support chat, or our Facebook Page: Soramma · Website: soramma.app
