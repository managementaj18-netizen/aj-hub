**Description (English):**
Fruit Catch is a fun, fast game for all ages. Slide your finger to move the basket and catch the falling fruit. Watch out for the creatures!

• Easy to learn: just drag the basket.
• Smiling fruit and golden pineapples give bonus points and coins.
• The deeper you go the harder it gets: meadow by day, forest at sunset, a quiet night with fireflies and finally a thunderstorm. Caterpillars, ants, bees, spiders, snakes and a dragon try to stop you.
• Waves: fruit showers and creature swarms break the rhythm.
• Earn coins and use the shop before a round: extra lives, a bigger basket or a magnet that pulls nearby fruit.
• Anonymous leaderboard: see your place and how many players reached each level. Challenge a friend by sharing your score.
• Khmer and English, music and sounds you can turn off. Plays without internet.
• No ads. No sign-up. No personal information needed.
