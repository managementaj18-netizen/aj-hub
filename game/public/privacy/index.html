🔒 Soramma Games · Privacy Policy

Applies to: Fruit Catch (play.soramma.app/fruit/), English Friends (play.soramma.app/abc/) and the Android app version of them. Last updated: November 2026.

English

Short version: our games have no ads, no sign-up, no email, no phone number, no location and no photos. We only keep a few anonymous numbers so we know how many people play.

What we collect

  • Anonymous visitor ID. A random code made on your phone and saved in the game's storage. It is not linked to your name, phone, account or device identity.
  • Simple usage counts. Visits, plays, shares and installs, and where the link came from (for example "Facebook"). Counted per day.
  • Score, and nickname (optional). Your best score is saved with your anonymous ID even if you type no name. A nickname is only saved if you type one and is used for challenging friends. If you share a challenge link, the name and score are inside that link, so anyone who opens it can read them. Your score is also saved on the server so a public leaderboard can show rank, level and score; the leaderboard never shows any names, other players appear only as "?". Only the game owner can see the saved names.
  • Game progress on your phone. Coins, best score, language, sound settings and shop items are saved only on your own device. If you clear the browser or app data, they are lost.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your IP address in our own database, your real name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Where data is kept

The website and its small database run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver the site and keep it secure, under its own privacy policy. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies.

Children

The games are made to be safe for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no chats between players, and no list of players' names.

Your choices

You can stop at any time and clear the game's data in your browser or app settings. To ask us to delete a nickname or score, contact us on our Facebook Page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
