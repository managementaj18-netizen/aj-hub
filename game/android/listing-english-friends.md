## English
**App name (max 30):** English Friends
**Short description (max 80):** Learn first English words with Momo the monkey. Listen, tap and spell.
**Full description (max 4000):**
English Friends helps young children learn their first English words in a friendly, colourful way. Momo the monkey tells a short story, you listen to the word, then tap the right picture.

• 6 story worlds: Farm, Fruits, Colors, Numbers, Family and Body — 36 words in all.
• Listen first: every word is spoken clearly, so children hear the right sound.
• Read, tap, spell: first the word is shown with the picture, then it gets a little harder, and finally children spell the word.
• Stars and stickers for every world you finish.
• Music and sounds can be turned off.
• No ads. No sign-up. No chat. No personal information needed.
