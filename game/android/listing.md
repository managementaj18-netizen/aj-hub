## English

**Short description (max 80):** Catch the falling fruit, dodge the bugs and go deeper into the forest.
**Full description (max 4000):**
Fruit Catch is a fun, fast game for all ages. Slide your finger to move the basket and catch the falling fruit. Watch out for the bugs!

• Easy to learn: just drag the basket.
• Gold pineapples give bonus points and coins.
• The deeper you go, the harder it gets: caterpillars, spiders, snakes and a dragon in the dark jungle. Fruit gives more coins too.
• Earn coins and use the shop before a round: extra lives, a bigger basket or a magnet that pulls nearby fruit (bugs are never pulled).
• Challenge a friend: share your best score as a link with a ready-made message.
• Khmer and English, music and sound effects you can turn off.
• No ads. No sign-up. No personal information needed.
